Privacy Policy

VayGo (Passenger App) · Last updated {{ lastUpdated }}

VayGo ("we", "us", "our") provides an on-demand ride-booking service that connects passengers with drivers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the VayGo passenger app, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the app, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide:

  • Your name, mobile number, gender, and (optionally) email address when you register.
  • Pickup and drop-off addresses, saved places, and ride preferences.
  • Promo/referral codes, ratings, and feedback you submit.
  • Messages you send to your driver or to support.

Information collected automatically:

  • Location. With your permission, we collect your device's precise GPS location to set your pickup point, show nearby drivers, track your ride in real time, and support safety features. You can disable location in your device settings, but core features will not work without it.
  • Device information (model, operating system, app version) and a push-notification token so we can send ride updates.
  • Ride history, fares, distances, and timestamps for each trip.
  • Basic diagnostic and usage data to keep the service reliable.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and verify you via one-time password (OTP).
  • To match you with a nearby driver and enable pickup, navigation, and drop-off.
  • To calculate fares, apply promotions, and process payments.
  • To share your live trip and driver details, and to enable the SOS/safety features.
  • To send ride status, receipts, and service notifications.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, and improve the service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Information Shared With Others

  • Drivers. To complete your ride, we share your first name, pickup and drop-off locations, and live location with the assigned driver. Your full phone number is not shown unless required for contact.
  • People you choose. When you share your live trip, the recipient can view your route and status via a link until the trip ends.
  • Service providers. We use trusted providers for maps and routing, notifications, and payments, who process data only on our behalf.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information to comply with law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of users and the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Location Data

Location is central to VayGo. We collect it while you are booking, waiting for, or on a ride so we can provide the service and safety features. We do not track your location when you are not using a location-dependent feature. You control location access through your device's permission settings.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and ride information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. You can request deletion of your account (see Contact).

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including secured connections and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

7. Your Rights & Choices

  • Access, correct, or update your profile information in the app.
  • Turn location, notifications, and other permissions on or off in device settings.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your personal data by contacting us.

8. Children

VayGo is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions or requests about this Policy or your data? Contact VayGo support through the in-app Support section, or email support@vaygotech.com.
